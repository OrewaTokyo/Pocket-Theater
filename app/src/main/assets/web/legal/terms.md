# Terms of use

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Pocket Theater is a free, open-source app (Apache-2.0 licence) that turns an Android phone into a private media server for your own network. There is no company behind it and no online service. These terms are for the person who runs the phone (the **admin**) and for everyone the admin approves.

## What Pocket Theater is

It shares the folders the admin picks, read-only, with approved people on the same Wi-Fi or hotspot. It does not come with any movies, series, anime, games or other content, and it does not check what is in the folders you share.

## Who can use it

- Only people the admin approves. The admin decides who joins and can turn off, delete or reset any account at any time, without giving a reason.
- Use it on your own network only. Never forward its ports to the internet and never use it to host files for the public.

## What you may share

- Share only files you own or have the right to share with the people you approve.
- Do not share anything that is illegal where you live. Pocket Theater is not a way to get around copyright or anyone's rights.

## The admin's responsibilities

- You are responsible for the folders you share, for the people you approve, and for keeping the phone and its PIN safe.
- Everyone you approve can see every shared folder. Approve only people you trust, and share only what they need.
- Follow the copyright and other laws that apply to you.

## Everyone else's responsibilities

- Keep your password private and do not let anyone else use your account.
- Do not try to get past the limits: guessing passwords, reaching folders that are not shared, or overloading the phone.
- Do not copy, record or pass on what you watch without the owner's permission.

## Privacy

What the app stores and what it can see is explained in the Privacy policy. The short version: everything stays on the phone and nothing is sent to the developer.

## When it is not available

The theater runs on a phone. It can stop when the phone is switched off, the battery runs out, Wi-Fi drops or Android closes the app. Features can change between versions.

## No warranty

Pocket Theater is provided "as is", without any promise that it works, is safe or is right for you. The developer is not responsible for lost data, damage, or the content that people share with it. The app only reads your files and never edits or deletes them, but keep your own backups anyway. The licence it is released under (Apache-2.0, sections 7 and 8) has the full wording on warranty and liability.

## Stopping

The admin can stop at any time by closing the theater or uninstalling the app. Anyone else can stop using it whenever they like.
